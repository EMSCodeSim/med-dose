# Clinical data review items

This file records items that require department / medical-director verification rather than an automated clinical change.

## Protocol baseline

The medication audit uses the Denver Metro EMS Medical Directors July 2026 protocol set already linked by the application. Department-specific waivers, formulary choices, concentrations, and implementation dates still require local approval.

## Ketamine

The app identifies Ketamine analgesia as department waiver `500:62`, not as a standard 9000-series Denver Metro medication protocol. The existing Ketamine dose, concentration, administration, and repeat logic were not changed during the July 2026 DMP audit. Verify the current department waiver before final approval or publication.

## Epinephrine push-dose preparation

Denver Metro protocol 9120 specifies agency-specific mixing guidance for adult push-dose Epinephrine. The app must not invent a universal department concentration. Confirm the department-approved preparation/mixing method in Admin and during the three-signature medication review.

## Department concentrations

Medication stock concentrations can vary by agency and supplier. The Admin clinical editor supports department-level concentration records, but every concentration change must be checked against the physical medication and local formulary before completing the three-signature review.
