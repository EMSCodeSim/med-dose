Tranexamic Acid (TXA)

Department Protocol 500:63 • Traumatic Hemorrhagic Shock

CURRENT DEPARTMENT DOSE

2 g IV
Infuse over 10 minutes.
Dose update: the supplied legacy 500:63 sheet lists a 1 g dose. Current department direction is 2 g, which supersedes that older dose line. Use the current department-approved preparation or premix and verify the final concentration before administration.

INDICATIONS — ALL REQUIRED

  • Age 18 years or older.
  • Acute trauma occurred less than 3 hours ago.
  • Suspected hemorrhagic shock due to trauma.
  • Signs of poor perfusion are present.

CONTRAINDICATIONS

  • Patient under 18 years of age.
  • More than 3 hours since injury.
  • Known hypersensitivity to tranexamic acid or a product ingredient.
  • Pregnancy.

ADMINISTRATION

  • Give 2 g by IV infusion over 10 minutes.
  • Do not administer as a rapid undiluted IV push.
  • If using 1 g/10 mL vials, 2 g contains 20 mL of source medication; prepare the final infusion according to current department practice.
  • Verify the physical medication label and final concentration before administration.

MONITORING / PRECAUTIONS

  • Monitor blood pressure and perfusion during administration; overly rapid IV administration may cause hypotension.
  • Serious thromboembolic complications have been reported with TXA.
  • Reassess clinical response and adverse effects after administration.
  • Transport patients receiving TXA to a Level I or II Trauma Center unless unable to oxygenate or ventilate per department trauma triage guidance.
